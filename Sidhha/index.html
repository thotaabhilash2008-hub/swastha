Forms of medicine

Tradition meets lab

Tradition
Nilavembu Kudineer, a herbal decoction, has long been used in fevers and was widely distributed in Tamil Nadu during dengue outbreaks. Kabasura Kudineer was promoted during COVID-19.
Lab
Nilavembu is Andrographis paniculata. Research focuses on andrographolide. Evidence for specific disease claims remains limited.
