Kalium Phosphoricum

Kali-p · The Great Nerve Remedy

Common namePhosphate of Potash, Dipotassium phosphate
Chemical formulaK₂HPO₄
FamilyMinerals
Introduced byDr. W.H. Schussler (tissue salt)
❧

Coloured glows on the body mark the pathology, what goes wrong in Kali-p. Tap a number to compare normal vs diseased.

❧

Mentals

  • Nervous temperament: anxiety, nervousness, mental fatigue
  • Cowardly, cannot narrate complaints without weeping
  • Clings to people and wants to be held; aversion to husband
  • Fear of being alone; frightful delusions and images
  • Weak memory and forgetfulness; dullness of the senses
  • Quarrelsome; startles from touch or noise
  • Sleeplessness from cares, worries, mental labour

Thought cells

  • Despondency, homesickness
  • Agarophobia, fearfulness, suspiciousness
  • Diminished neuron activity gives forgetfulness

Physicals

  • Anaemic, chlorotic (green sickness), delicate persons worn out by long suffering
  • Obese, easily exhausted people with lax fibre and offensive body odour
  • Thermal: worse from cold
  • Diathesis: nervous, cancerous
  • Regional: nerves, brain, excretions

Action on nerves

  • Vasomotor: small, frequent pulse first, then retarded
  • Sensory: pain, with a sensation of paralysis
  • Motor: weakness of muscles up to paralysis
  • Trophic: retarded nutrition, softening; treats depression
  • Mucous membrane discharges: blackish, non-coagulating

Aggravation: worse from

  • Excitement
  • Worry
  • Physical fatigue
  • Dry air
  • Coition
  • Puberty
  • Cold

Amelioration: better from

  • Eating
  • Sleep
  • Leaning against a support

Compare: Sil, Stram, Hyos, Arg-n, Ars, Podo, Tub, Caust, Anac, Ign. Study Kali-p and Calc-p if Phosphorus fails.
Compatible: Ferr-p, Calc-f, Mag-p, Cycl.
