Ilaj bit Tadbeer: regimenal therapy

Treatment by method rather than by medicine. These are classical regimens, performed by trained practitioners.

Graded exercise, matched to age, season and temperament.

The Silk Road of medicine

Hippocrates and Galen
Greek physicians who shaped the four-humor model.

Al-Razi, 9th to 10th century
Persian physician known for clinical observation and careful case records.

Al-Zahrawi, c. 936 to 1013
Surgeon of Córdoba whose Kitab al-Tasrif illustrated surgical instruments.

Ibn Sina, 980 to 1037
Wrote Al-Qanun fi'l-Tibb (the Canon of Medicine), a standard text for centuries.

Hakim Ajmal Khan, 1868 to 1927
Revived Unani in India and led the Tibbiya College in Delhi.

Hyderabad
Home to Nizamia Tibbi College and the Central Research Institute of Unani Medicine.
