Classical AYUSH Herbarium Specimen

Tulasi (Holy Basil)

Ocimum tenuiflorum / Ocimum sanctum

Celebrated across classical Charaka and Sushruta Samhita as 'Surasa' and the 'Queen of Herbs'. Revered as a prime Rasayana that balances Vata and Kapha doshas, purifies the Pranavaha Srotas, and fortifies respiratory vitality.

Rasa (Taste): Katu (Pungent), Tikta (Bitter)
Guna (Quality): Laghu (Light), Ruksha (Dry)
Virya (Potency): Ushna (Heating)
Vipaka (Post-Digest): Katu (Pungent)
Key Therapeutic Actions
Respiratory Vitality Immunity Booster Adaptogen & Stress Antimicrobial
