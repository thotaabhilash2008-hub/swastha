Known as Sarva Roga Nivarini — "the curer of all ailments" — Neem has been an indispensable pillar of Indian domestic and Ayurvedic medicine for more than four millennia. Every part of the tree possesses therapeutic virtue, earning it the title of The Village Pharmacy across South Asia.

01

Botanical Profile

Azadirachta indica is a fast-growing, drought-resistant evergreen tree of the mahogany family (Meliaceae), reaching heights of 15–30 metres. Native to the Indian subcontinent, it thrives in arid regions and is characterised by a dense, rounded crown, deeply fissured grey-brown bark, and fragrant honey-scented white flowers borne in axillary panicles.

02

Phytochemistry Composition

Neem is among the most phytochemically complex medicinal plants known to science, containing over 400 structurally diverse bioactives. Its primary therapeutic metabolites are tetranortriterpenoids known as limonoids — notably azadirachtin, nimbin, nimbidin, and salannin — which exhibit potent antimicrobial, antidiabetic, and anti-inflammatory activities.

03

Ayurvedic Significance

Classical Ayurveda classifies Nimba as the premier Kaphapittashamaka (pacifier of Kapha and Pitta doshas) and one of the supreme blood purifiers (Raktashodhaka). Charaka classifies it in the Kandughna (antipruritic) and Kushtaghna (antidermatosis) groups.

04

Key Health Benefits

Dermatological Healing & Acne
Nimbidin and nimbolide inhibit Propionibacterium acnes and Staphylococcus aureus, reducing pustular lesions, eczema, psoriasis, and promoting collagen re-epithelialisation.
Systemic Blood Purification
Acts on the liver-spleen axis to filter metabolic endotoxins (Ama), clearing circulating inflammatory cytokines and enhancing cutaneous radiance.
Oral Hygiene & Plaque Control
Azadirachtin binds bacterial cell membranes in oral Streptococcus mutans. Traditional chewing twigs (datun) prevent gingivitis, caries, and halitosis.
Glycemic & Metabolic Balance
Ethanolic leaf extracts enhance peripheral glucose utilisation, stimulate insulin release from pancreatic beta-cells, and protect renal arterioles.
Antifungal & Antimicrobial
Broad-spectrum efficacy against Candida albicans, Trichophyton mentagrophytes (ringworm), and multi-drug resistant biofilm formers.
Immunomodulation
Polysaccharides and polyphenols stimulate cell-mediated immune responses, increasing macrophage phagocytosis and CD4+/CD8+ lymphocyte populations.

Safety & Precautions

  • Pregnancy & Lactation: Avoid internal consumption. Traditional texts and modern studies report emmenagogue properties and anti-implantation effects.
  • Hypoglycemia: Potentiates insulin and oral hypoglycemic agents. Monitor blood glucose closely.
  • Children: Internal use of raw neem seed oil is strictly contraindicated in infants due to risk of Reye-like hepatic stress.
  • Autoimmune Conditions: Immune-stimulating bioactives may stimulate symptom flare-ups in lupus or rheumatoid arthritis if taken unmonitored.
